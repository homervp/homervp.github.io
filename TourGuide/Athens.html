Homer's Tour Guide to Athens

Museums

- Acropolis Museum - Benaki museum (folk) - National Historical Museum (Old Parliament) - Numismatic (judging by Corfu’s) - Of Cycladic Art

Archaeological sites

- Acropolis (of course) o Parthenon o Gates of Hermes o Heraion o Erechthion - Odeion of Herod of Attica - Ancient Agora - Roman Agora - Arch of Hadrian - Columns of Olympian Zeus - Filopappou hill o Socrates’ prison - Lyceum of Aristotle - Theater of Dionysus Eleutherus

Historical places

- Armored Cruiser Averof o Trireme Olympias (?) o Floating Museum “Neraida” - Parliament (Syntagma square) - Monastiraki Square o Hadrian’s Library - Zappeion garden / National Garden

Culture

- Gazi & Technopolis (exhibitions, music festivals) - Theatro Vrachon (Melina Merkouri Theater) - Megaron / Athens Concert Hall / Megaro Mousikis - Thiseion Open Air Cinema

Areas

- Kolonaki - Kerameikos/Gazi - Plaka - Jacqueline de Romilly square – drinks/food with a view (also the rooftops on the side)

Food

- Lukumades - Romanakia (far) - Tzitzikas & Mermigkas (chain) - President Hotel rooftop - Bairaktaris (Monastiraki or Syntagma square) - Nancy’s Sweet Home / Serbetospito of Nancy - Konstantinidis pastry shops - Terkenlis pastry shops - Klimataria

Day Trips

- Aegina (40 mins from port, ~2h overhead each way including metro/waiting) - Aerchaeological site of Sounio - Corinth (ancient Corinth + Akrocorinth) (1h by car) - Hydra - Nafplion - Mykines / Mycinae - Delphi
